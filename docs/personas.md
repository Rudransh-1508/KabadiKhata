# Personas

Two people sit on opposite sides of Kabadi Khata. Every feature serves one of them.

## Raju — the kabadiwala (primary user)
- Collects plastic, paper, metal and e-waste door-to-door in **Lajpat Nagar**, ~150 kg on a good day.
- Sells to an aggregator/recycler for **cash**. Nothing he does is on record.
- Has a basic Android phone and WhatsApp; **low literacy**, reads Hinglish far more comfortably than Devanagari or English.
- **Problem for him:** the 2026 rules push unregistered collectors out of the formal chain, and he has no proof of what he collects — so no record for a loan (e.g. PM SVANidhi-style micro-credit) and no claim to EPR-grade value.
- **What Kabadi Khata gives him:** send a photo + a voice note (or tap buttons) → a verified, tamper-evident receipt with a fair indicative price, and a shareable **income statement** ("Mera Hisaab").

## Priya — the brand's EPR officer (second-side user)
- Works for a company that must buy EPR recycling certificates to meet its obligations.
- **Problem for her:** certificates can be fake — in 2024, CPCB audits of four plastic recyclers turned up ~6 lakh fake EPR certificates — and today she **cannot tell** which certificate is backed by plastic a collector actually handed over.
- **What Kabadi Khata gives her:** each certificate shows a **verified-backed %** (how much of the claimed tonnage traces to real, chain-verified receipts), so she can refuse anything below threshold. She sees provenance **in aggregate only** — Cedar denies her the kabadiwalas' identities.

## The auditor (operator, shown in the console)
- Runs the deterministic **fraud radar**, reviews flags with evidence, and clicks *Request inspection*.
- Code decides the flag; the LLM only explains it; the auditor approves the action.

> Pitch-line discipline (CLAUDE.md #8): the 6-lakh figure is the 2024 NGT/CPCB notice; the **38×** case is **our simulation**, never a CPCB finding.
